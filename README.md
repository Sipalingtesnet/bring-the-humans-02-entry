## What is make.fun

make.fun is a competitive bounty arena where humans and their AI coding agents
collaborate to build real software for cash prizes. Every bounty provides:

- **A clear task:** Specifications and requirements for what needs to be built.
- **A deadline:** A hard closing time for submissions.
- **A cash reward:** Prizes paid directly in USDC on the Solana network.

You work hand-in-hand with your preferred AI coding agent. The agent handles
the heavy lifting of writing code, while you direct the vision, review the
outputs, test the application, and capture your session transcripts. Entries
are graded fairly against published rules.

For this specific bounty (**bring-the-humans-02**), the prize pool is **$1,000**,
with **50 paid winning places**, and entry is **100% free**. Check out the live
bounty details at https://make.fun/bounty/bring-the-humans-02

## What you need

Getting started takes just a few standard tools:

- **A Solana Wallet:** To receive your USDC earnings (or sign in via GitHub,
  Google, Apple, or Discord and connect a wallet later).
- **A GitHub Account:** Your submission repository will be hosted publicly on
  GitHub.
- **An AI Coding Agent:** Whatever tool you currently use to generate and
  refine code with AI.

## Step 1 — Create your account

1. Go to https://make.fun in your browser.

## Step 2 — Pick a bounty

Navigate to the bounties page and review active competitions:

- **Read the task specification thoroughly.** Understand what is being asked.
- **Note the deadline.** Give yourself ample time to build and test.
- **Check the prize distribution.** This bounty features a **$1,000** pool across
  **50 paid places**.
- **Inspect the provided kit.**

## Step 3 — Get the kit

1. Download the bounty kit directly from the bounty page.
2. Open it locally and **read `RULES.txt` or the kit README first** before doing
   anything else. The rules dictate how entries are judged.
3. Set up your workspace so your AI agent has direct access to the kit files.

## Step 4 — Build your entry with your AI agent

1. Open your AI coding agent in a clean session.
2. Provide the kit and prompt the agent to implement the required solution.
3. Act as the lead architect: review the generated code, test functionality,
   and guide iterative improvements.
4. **Capture your transcripts.** Your agent session logs serve as authentic
   proof of work and should be preserved in your submission repo.
5. Run local tests to ensure the software works reliably.

## Step 5 — Submit your entry and transcripts

1. Push your finished code and your AI session transcripts to a public GitHub
   repository.
2. Navigate to the bounty page on make.fun, enter your repository URL and the
   specific commit hash.
3. Verify the link is accessible and public before submitting.

## Step 6 — Check status, fix, and resubmit

1. Check your submission status on the bounty page after submitting.
2. If feedback indicates an issue or missing requirement, address it promptly
   in your repository.
3. Commit the fix and resubmit before the deadline. You can update your
   submission multiple times.

## Step 7 — Claim your reward

1. Once judging concludes, check the results leaderboard.
2. If you placed in the winners circle, claim your USDC on Solana within the
   specified claim window.

## Tips for your first win

- **Follow rules strictly:** Grading is based on adherence to the kit rules.
- **Start early:** Avoid last-minute deployment panic.
- **Keep submissions clean:** Simple, fully working code beats complex breakage.
- **Preserve transcripts:** Always include your agent interaction logs.
